\begin{abstract}
Chunked prefill bounds the prefill work admitted into an iteration, but the
service share between prefill and decode remains implicit in mixed batches.
This is restrictive when one tensor-parallel replica must serve both phases
and neither another replica nor intra-device partitioning is available. We
present PD-TDM, which retains bounded prefill and local KV state while
scheduling prefill-only and decode-only iterations with an explicit phase
share. On Qwen3-8B using two Ascend 910B3 NPUs, PD-TDM improves strict
joint-SLO attainment over same-platform vLLM-Ascend chunked prefill by 34.8
percentage points on a conversation burst and 73.9 points on a code burst at
representative high loads. A reconstructed MaaS trace shows lower mean/P99
time to first token. Decode-heavy tests expose the boundary: benefits diminish
or reverse when decode has little latency slack.
\end{abstract}
