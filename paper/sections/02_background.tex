\section{Background and Related Work}
\label{sec:background}

\textbf{Iteration-level and mixed-batch serving.}
Orca established iteration-level scheduling, and vLLM made continuous batching
practical with PagedAttention~\cite{yu2022orca,kwon2023vllm}. Sarathi-Serve
introduced bounded Prefill chunks and decode-maximal mixed batches to reduce
generation stalls~\cite{agrawal2024sarathi}; DeepSpeed-FastGen follows the same
broad direction by splitting and combining prompt and generation work
~\cite{holmes2024fastgen}. PD-TDM retains bounded Prefill, but changes the
control surface: instead of selecting the P/D composition of every mixed
iteration, it selects which phase receives the next service quantum. Our
measured baseline is the native vLLM-Ascend chunked-prefill path on the same
hardware, not a cross-platform Sarathi-Serve reimplementation.

\textbf{Disaggregation and SLO-oriented allocation.}
Splitwise and DistServe separate Prefill and Decode to reduce interference and
optimize TTFT/TPOT-oriented goodput~\cite{patel2024splitwise,
zhong2024distserve}; TetriInfer, P/D-Serve, and DejaVu study related
disaggregated execution and KV movement problems~\cite{hu2024tetrinfer,
jin2024pdserve,strati2024dejavu}. Mooncake makes KV storage and movement a
first-class distributed serving resource~\cite{qin2025mooncake}. TaiChi is the
closest prior work in high-level objective: it combines aggregation and
disaggregation with heterogeneous P-heavy/D-heavy instances and request-level
latency shifting~\cite{wang2025taichi}. We share the dual-SLO perspective, but
not the control object. TaiChi places requests across heterogeneous instances;
PD-TDM keeps the topology fixed and allocates the next iteration to a phase on
one TP replica. We therefore do not claim SLO-aware goodput as a new
objective.

\textbf{Spatial and temporal multiplexing.}
MuxServe studies flexible spatial-temporal placement for multiple model services
\cite{duan2024muxserve}. MuxWise, semi-PD, Nexus, Bullet, and DuetServe explore
unified storage, intra-device partitioning, or dynamic spatial-temporal
orchestration~\cite{chen2026muxwise,hong2025semipd,shi2025nexus,
lin2026bullet,gao2026duetserve}. EcoServe is especially close because it uses
temporal phase orchestration, but coordinates role changes across cooperating
instances in a commodity-GPU cluster~\cite{du2026ecoserve}. PD-TDM does not
claim to originate temporal disaggregation. Its narrower question is whether
bounded, iteration-granular phase allocation provides a useful TTFT--TPOT
operating point when both phases remain on one TP replica and extra replicas or
spatial partitioning are outside the deployment budget.
