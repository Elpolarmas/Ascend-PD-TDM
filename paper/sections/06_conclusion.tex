\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

PD-TDM separates a bounded prefill chunk from the cross-iteration service share
between prefill and decode. It keeps weights and KV state in one shared TP
replica while scheduling phase-pure iterations. On two Ascend 910B3 NPUs, this
design improves joint-SLO attainment for prefill-heavy bursts and shifts TTFT
left on a reconstructed MaaS trace. Decode-heavy counterexamples provide the
necessary qualification: temporal phase isolation is useful when decode has
latency slack, and can lose when TPOT is already tight.
