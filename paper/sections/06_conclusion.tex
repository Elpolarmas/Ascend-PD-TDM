\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

PD-TDM exposes cross-iteration Prefill/Decode allocation on a single
tensor-parallel replica through bounded phase-pure iterations, a token-bucket
selector, and SLO-pressure feedback. On two Ascend 910B3 NPUs, the integrated
path improves joint-SLO goodput in Prefill-pressure bursts and shifts TTFT on a
fixed-ratio MaaS replay, while exploratory boundaries show no universal
advantage. These results support the integrated policy under the tested
configuration, not controller-only causality or an optimal ratio.
